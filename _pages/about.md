Welcome to my website! I received my PhD in Political Science from Claremont Graduate University. My research focuses on American political thought and history, U.S. constitutional law and institutions, and race and ethnic politics. I am particularly interested in the American Founding, constitutionalism, American slavery and its legacy, and rhetoric. I study American political thought in the context of the broader tradition of Western political philosophy. I also conduct empirical research using administrative, public opinion, and textual data.
