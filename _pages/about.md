Welcome to my website! I received my PhD in Political Science from Claremont Graduate University. My research focuses on American political thought and history, U.S. constitutional law and institutions, and race and ethnic politics. I am particularly interested in the American Founding, constitutionalism, American slavery and its legacy, and rhetoric. I study American political thought in relation to the larger tradition of western political philosophy. My theoretical interests include the works of the American Founders, Abraham Lincoln, and Frederick Douglass, among others. I also conduct empirical research using administrative, public opinion, and textual data.
