Welcome to my website! I received my PhD in Political Science from Claremont Graduate University. My research lies at the intersection of American politics, political theory, and public law, with a particular focus on American political thought, institutions, and development. I am especially interested in American constitutionalism, American national institutions and their development, American slavery, representation, the separation of powers, federalism, polarization, and populism. I use historical and political economy approaches to answer important questions in American politics.
