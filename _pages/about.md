Welcome to my website! I hold a Ph.D. in Political Science from Claremont Graduate University, specializing in Political Theory and American Politics. My research focuses on American political thought, race and ethnic politics, and political psychology. I am particularly interested in the American Founding, American slavery and its legacy, racial stereotyping and prejudice, and racial threat. My theoretical interests include the works of the American Founders, Abraham Lincoln, and Frederick Douglass, among others. I also conduct empirical research using administrative and public opinion data.
