Welcome to my website! I received my PhD in Political Science from Claremont Graduate University. My research lies at the intersection of American politics, political theory, and public law, with a particular focus on American constitutionalism and political thought, and American political institutions and development. I am especially interested in the founding principles of American government, American slavery, Progressivism, representation, polarization, and populism. I use theoretical, historical, and empirical approaches to answer important questions in American politics.
